# 사진과 지형 자료

The 해무각 hero uses an edited version of 〈해운대 해무가 걸린 풍경〉, taken by 부산광역시 in 2020 and made available under [공공누리 제1유형](https://www.kogl.or.kr/recommend/recommendDivView.do?division=img&recommendIdx=14737). The edit adds denser sea fog on the right.
The viewpoint map uses three 부산광역시 archive photos from 2020 under 공공누리 제1유형. [해운대 해무가 걸린 풍경](https://www.kogl.or.kr/recommend/recommendDivView.do?division=img&recommendIdx=14737), [광안리 해무](https://www.kogl.or.kr/recommend/recommendDivView.do?division=img&recommendIdx=14655), [광안대교 해무](https://www.kogl.or.kr/recommend/recommendDivView.do?division=img&recommendIdx=14648).
The photos shown for 엘시티 and 달맞이언덕 were provided by the user.

The 3D terrain uses [USGS SRTM elevations via Mapzen Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/).
